FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Common questions regarding AttendX smart-campus deployment and features.

How does AttendX dispatch notifications without SMS API costs?
AttendX features a dual-dispatch system: in standalone deployment, it utilizes high-speed reactive browser pub/sub with audio synthesis and local state synchronization. In enterprise setup, it easily hooks to Twilio, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), or WhatsApp Business APIs.
What happens when a student's attendance falls below 75%?
The platform automatically activates the Autonomous Risk Guard. The student's dashboard displays a pulsing debarment warning along with a calculated recovery roadmap (e.g., "Attend 5 consecutive lectures to regain eligibility"), while guardians receive a formal attendance alert.
Can parents view past attendance from previous weeks?
Yes! The Parent Portal features an interactive monthly calendar where every day is color-coded (Green for Present, Red for Absent, Yellow for Late). Clicking any date reveals the exact timetable, lecture topics, and professor notes for that day.
